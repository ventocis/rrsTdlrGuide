# Operator grouping — review sheet

Generated 2026-10-13 from `tdlr-source/operator-groups.csv` and
`operator-merge-candidates.csv`. Nothing here is applied yet. Phase 2 research
is sized off whatever you decide, so this is the gate.

## What the grouping found

471 current driving-safety licences resolve to **158 operators**: 16 multi-brand
operators holding 329 licences between them, plus 142 genuine single-licence
schools. The site already covers 134 of the 158.

So the real backlog is **22 new operators plus 2 partially listed** — about 24
to research, not the 159 absent licences. Three worth naming:

| Operator | Licences | Listed today |

| FAST EASY DEFENSIVE DRIVING | 119 | 39 |
| TICKET DISMISSAL TEXAS | 46 | **0 — absent entirely** |
| COURTAPPROVEDCOMEDYDRIVINGSAFETY.COM | 22 | 10 |

`TICKET DISMISSAL TEXAS` holding 46 current licences and appearing nowhere on the
site is the single biggest gap in the directory.

## Decision 1 — eight shared addresses (yes/no each)

Grouping keyed on phone number. Street address suggests eight further merges.
I did not apply them, because the answer genuinely differs case to case.

### Recommend MERGE — white-label shells of one operator

| # | Address | Brands | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Milpitas 95035 | 16 groups incl. `URBANTRAFFICSCHOOL.COM` and fourteen `$25 … ONLINE VIDEO COURSE` variants | One operator, sixteen storefronts, separate phone each |
| 2 | Austin 78727 (Parmer) | `VIDEO - EZ 100% PASS…` + `-- VIDEO -- CHEAPER…` | Same template, same suite |
| 3 | Houston 77057 (Fountain View) | `VIDEO- FREE CERTIFICATE…` + `EASYPASSDEFENSIVEDRIVING.COM` (21) | Same |
| 4 | San Antonio 78209 (Broadway) | `LOWEST PRICE - FREE CERTIFICATE…` + `-- VIDEO -- CHEAPER…` | Same |
| 5 | Plano 75025 (Stanmore) | `FAST TRACK DEFENSIVE DRIVING` (4) + `CHEAP AND EASY DEFENSIVE DRIVING` | Same |

**One caveat on 2 and 4.** The brand `-- VIDEO -- CHEAPER -- FASTER -- EASIER --`
appears at *both* the Austin and San Antonio addresses. Approving both merges
therefore chains Austin and San Antonio into a single operator. That is probably
right — one operator with two mail drops — but it is a transitive merge, which
the grouping deliberately avoids doing on its own. Say so explicitly if you want
it.

### Recommend DO NOT MERGE — one owner, but competing on price

| # | Address | Brands | Why not |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Austin 78701 (610 W 5th) | `ACEABLE DEFENSIVE DRIVING` + `I DRIVE SAFELY` | Aceable's HQ. Same owner, but they sell at $49 and $29 |
| 7 | Houston 77068 (FM 1960) | `DRIVERSED.COM` + `I DRIVE SAFELY` | Same family; DriversEd.com sells at $34 |
| 8 | Orlando 32801 | `AMERICAN SAFETY COUNCIL` + `AAA CONTINUING EDUCATION` + `RUSHDEFENSIVEDRIVING.COM` | American Safety Council is the parent, but AAA is a brand a shopper recognises and would expect to see on its own |

6 and 7 are the same corporate family — Aceable, I Drive Safely, DriversEd.com —
at three different prices. Collapsing them into one row would hide two real
options from someone comparing cost, which is the whole job of the page. Better
to list them separately and, if you want, note shared ownership in the expanded
row.

## Decision 2 — one operator split across two site rows

`ONLINE DEFENSIVE DRIVING SCHOOLS` holds 7 licences but occupies **2 separate
rows** on the site today. One of them should absorb the other. Low risk, just
needs a nod.

## Decision 3 — display names

Fourteen of the Milpitas brands are literally named things like
`$$$ $25 $$$ - ONLINE VIDEO COURSE- UNLIMITED ATTEMPTS`. If merged, the group
needs one display name. `URBANTRAFFICSCHOOL.COM` is the only real brand in the
set and is my suggestion. Same question for `FAST EASY DEFENSIVE DRIVING` (119
licences) — it keeps the name it already has on the site unless you say otherwise.
